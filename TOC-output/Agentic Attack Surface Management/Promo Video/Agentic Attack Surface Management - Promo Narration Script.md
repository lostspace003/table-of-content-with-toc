# Agentic Attack Surface Management - promo narration

Voice: the presenter's own cloned voice. Duration: 5.1 min.

**[hook]** You cannot protect what you cannot find. Forgotten subdomains. Expired certificates. Cloud resources nobody owns.

**[hook]** What does your attack surface look like to an attacker, right now? In this course you build the platform that answers that question every day, without ever stepping outside authorized scope.

**[intro]** Gennoor Tech presents Agentic Attack Surface Management.

**[story]** Discover. Reconcile. Assign. Five sources, D N S, certificates, cloud inventory, C M D B and scanner findings, reconciled into one owned asset graph. Every record carries provenance. Every uncertain match goes to a review queue. And every discovery action is checked against scope before it runs.

**[path0]** Day one starts with authorization, because weak scope is the biggest risk in attack surface tooling. You write the allowlist, the exclusions, the rate limits and the audit trail, and you prove that an out-of-scope target is blocked and logged.

**[path0]** Then the five source adapters, fixture-based and testable, and a normalized inventory with canonical asset I Ds, first and last seen, confidence, environment and relationships.

**[path1]** Day two is reconciliation. Entity resolution uses deterministic signals, exact hostname, certificate S A N, cloud resource I D and verified ownership. Anything uncertain is queued for review, never silently merged. Then change detection: snapshot to snapshot, you find what is new, changed, missing, stale or orphaned, validated against fixtures with known answers.

**[path2]** Then exposure. Assets join to findings, service metadata and business criticality, and an agent summarizes the exposure. It must separate what was observed from what it inferred, and you measure how often it fails to.

**[path3]** Day three is ownership and action. Synthetic C M D B records, tags and repository metadata suggest a responsible team, with evidence attached and manual correction built in. Transparent ranking raises review tickets through a mock connector. And you publish the metrics: link precision, unowned asset rate, stale classification and change-detection accuracy.

**[labs]** Every lab uses fixture sources and lab-owned domains, so the discovery you practise is real in technique and safe in scope.

**[mods]** Here is the full table of contents, 9 modules over three days. Day one: Authorization, scope policy and the lab; Source adapters; and Asset normalization and canonical IDs. Day two: Entity resolution; Change detection; and Exposure correlation with an agent. Day three: Ownership resolution; Prioritization, ticketing and inventory quality metrics; and Capstone: scoped attack surface platform.

**[princ]** Every build in this course follows six engineering principles. Model output is a proposal, never a fact. Authorization lives outside the model. A human approves every consequential action. Decisions and evidence are logged. The stack stays provider-agnostic. And quality is measured, not assumed.

**[stats]** Five asset sources. Three days, hands-on. Zero out-of-scope discovery. One owned asset graph.
