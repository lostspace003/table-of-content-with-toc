**[hook]** Your network team runs on tickets and the command line. Certificates to renew. Firmware to upgrade. Health checks, troubleshooting, change records. Every single week.

**[hook]** Most of that work follows a pattern. Gather the evidence. Decide. Execute. Verify. Document. What if Claude did the first eighty percent of it, safely, and your engineers only approved the decisions that matter?

**[intro]** Gennoor Tech presents Automating I T and Network Operations with Claude.

**[story]** Eleven real workflows. One method. Claude plans and drafts. Your code executes. A human approves. Every automation in this course is built the same way, so by the end you own a repeatable pattern, not a pile of scripts.

**[path0]** First, the foundations. You set up Claude Code, the Claude Agent S D K and M C P servers against a lab network, and build your first read-only device agent in the first hour.

**[path0]** Then guardrails. Least privilege, dry-run, diffs, approval gates and an audit trail that records what Claude saw, what it proposed, who approved it and what actually ran. That library is reused in every module that follows.

**[path0]** Still on day one, you automate certificate renewal across F5 BIG-IP, Citrix NetScaler, Radware and other load balancers, and build a health monitor that only opens a ServiceNow ticket when a state really changes.

**[path1]** Day two is device operations. Firmware upgrades with space clean-up, pre-checks, post-checks, a scheduler and automatic rollback. Cisco A C I upgrades following Cisco's recommended process, with Claude producing the operator runbook stage by stage.

**[path1]** And troubleshooting that starts in ServiceNow. The incident lands, Claude acknowledges it, asks for the primary details, runs read-only C L I checks on the right platform, and suggests the next action. Every resolution becomes a reusable template of reason, action and next action.

**[path2]** Then change and migration. You compare configurations across platforms, cloud to cloud, physical to cloud, and get a real gap report. You generate traffic flow diagrams from traceroute and ping. And you learn Terraform step by step, with the common mistakes, inside a GitHub workflow where Claude reviews every pull request before a gated apply.

**[path3]** Day three widens the lens to the business. Credential and access onboarding for new joiners, with tickets raised on their behalf and secrets kept out of the model entirely. An H R hiring flow that screens for English proficiency, experience, location, salary ask and working hours, fairly and with consent. And bill creation straight from the mailbox, with G S T and non G S T variants and an exceptions queue for anything Claude is not sure about.

**[labs]** Every lab runs against simulated devices and real lab tenants, so you can break things. You will see the agent refuse an unsafe action, roll back a failed upgrade, and ignore a malicious instruction hidden in a ticket.

A human approves every consequential action. Decisions and evidence are logged. The stack stays provider-agnostic. And quality is measured, not assumed.
